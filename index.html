3 · Simulations

Bulk Simulation

Runs a hypothetical streak from a fresh 0-task record, unlocking modifiers along the way. Picks the best hearts/hour offer when one is on a good pace; otherwise clears the fastest trivial task; otherwise skips (thresholds under Simulations in Settings). This has no effect on your session.

Log

Task pool, weightings, quantities and modifier rules are from Jagex's news post “Meet Mortimer: Your Newest Slayer Master” (24 Jul 2026, revised 27 Jul). Default task ratings and kill speeds follow Travag's pre-release Mortimer spreadsheet (grades mapped to Elo; kills/hr from average quantity ÷ task time), assume high-level gear, and are editable in Settings. Imbued heart odds use each superior's exact drop rate from the wiki's Imbued heart page (requires Bigger & Badder; superior spawns at 1/150 with the elite CA setting on, 1/200 otherwise). Clue estimates assume a 1/128 base rate. Bracelet toggles apply Expeditious (×0.8 actual kills) to tasks where fewer kills is preferable and Slaughter (×4/3) where more is, per their wiki effects.
